    What is a wa.me link and why use a generator?

    WhatsApp offers an official short link format, wa.me, that opens a chat with a specific number directly. The structure is simple: https://wa.me/ followed by the number in international format (country code and number, digits only) and, optionally, ?text= with the message encoded so it works inside a URL.

    Building it by hand leads to easy mistakes: leaving the “+” or leading zeros, forgetting the country code, or typing accents, spaces and emojis without encoding them. This WhatsApp link generator with message handles everything: it cleans the number, encodes the text correctly and shows you the result before you share it.

    Tips to make sure your custom WhatsApp QR code always scans

    Always use a dark foreground on a light background: QR readers rely on contrast, and inverted or pastel codes can fail on older phones. If you add your logo, the tool automatically switches to maximum error correction. For print, download the SVG (vector, no quality loss) and don't print it smaller than 2 × 2 cm (about 0.8 in); long messages create denser codes that should be printed larger. Always test the code with your phone before sending it to print.

    Text formatting in the message

    WhatsApp supports basic formatting and keeps it in pre-filled messages: *bold*, _italic_, ~strikethrough~ and ```monospace```. Use the editor's formatting buttons and you'll see the result in the preview instantly.

    Does the WhatsApp QR code expire?

    No. The QR code contains your wa.me link directly, with no redirects in between, so it will work as long as your number is active on WhatsApp.

    Can I add my logo to a custom WhatsApp QR code?

    Yes. You can add a chat icon or upload your own logo (PNG, JPG or SVG). The tool automatically increases the code's error correction so it keeps scanning reliably.

    Yes. wa.me links work the same for personal accounts and WhatsApp Business: the chat opens with your number and the message already typed.

    Which QR format should I download, PNG or SVG?

    PNG is ideal for social media, websites and documents. SVG is a vector format that scales without losing quality, perfect for print, business cards, posters and vinyl.

    Why won't my QR code scan?

    The most common causes are low color contrast, a foreground lighter than the background, a print size that's too small or a very long message. Use a dark color on a light background and print it at least 2 × 2 cm (about 0.8 in).
